Before you plan around six sittings — that rule is not yours The “nine exams within six sittings” limit in FCL.025(b)(3) applies only to applicants for the ATPL theory, CPL or IR. For a LAPL or PPL, FCL.025(b)(4) imposes no limit on the number of sittings, and the UK CAA removed the sittings cap from its GA exam procedures in December 2020. You do not need to hold sittings in reserve. See for the quoted regulation.

How the syllabus data was produced

The nine CAP2090 PDFs were downloaded from caa.co.uk and parsed as tables. Each row’s PPL-Aeroplane tick column was read directly from the PDF table cell, giving 0 learning objectives of which 0 are ticked for PPL(A). Those two counts were cross-checked against an independent pass that classified tick marks by their x-coordinate on the page; both methods agreed exactly. No objective text was paraphrased.
